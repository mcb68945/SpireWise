What a line means: picking one card when the other is already in your deck changes your odds of winning by the amount shown, compared with picking it without that partner. That comes from offered-vs-skipped picks at A10, adjusted for floor, deck size and the rest of the deck, with small samples pulled toward zero. It is not "decks that won had both", which mostly rewards cards picked late in runs that were already winning.
